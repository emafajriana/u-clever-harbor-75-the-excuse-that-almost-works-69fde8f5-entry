Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty **The Excuse That Almost Works** — the one line a gamer texts the boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week, at most 20 words.

## The entry

> My building's lift is stuck with my work laptop inside and the engineer says he'll be here sometime before lunch.

113 characters, 20 words, one line. A mundane building failure with a second beat — the work laptop is stuck in the lift too — and a vague "sometime before lunch" that sounds like a real engineer. The kind of boring, specific detail a tired manager would almost believe.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and verifies it against the gate first: 1–140 characters, a single line, at most 20 words, with a byte-exact read-back after writing.
